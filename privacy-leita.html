2. In short

Leita does not process any personal data. The app never connects to the internet, has no user accounts, shows no advertising, contains no analytics or tracking services and requests no Android permissions. All puzzles and the scoring of your guesses are computed entirely on your device.

3. Data on your device

Settings, the current game state and your statistics are stored only in the app's protected storage on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to third parties. You can delete them at any time by uninstalling the app or by choosing "Apps → Leita → Storage → Clear data" in the Android settings.

4. Share function

When you tap "Share", Leita hands a short text (puzzle code and database version, never your guesses) to the app you pick in the share dialog, for example a messenger. Only with that choice does the text leave Leita; from then on the privacy policy of the chosen app applies. Without your choice nothing is transmitted.

5. App store and operating system

The app is distributed through Google Play (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). During download, updates and, depending on your device settings, crash or performance reports, Google processes data under its own privacy policy. From this we receive at most aggregated statistics without personal reference (for example install counts or crash frequency).

6. Your rights

Because we do not process personal data about you, we hold no data we could disclose or delete. Regardless of that, you have the rights under Articles 15 to 21 GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Please send requests to felixkohle.business@gmail.com.

7. Support requests

If you e-mail us, we process the data contained in your message (sender address, content, attachments) solely to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We delete it no later than twelve months after the request is closed unless a statutory retention duty applies.

8. Changes

This policy is updated when the app or the legal situation changes. The current version is available at this address and linked from the app's settings.
